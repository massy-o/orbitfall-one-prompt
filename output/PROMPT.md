# The One Prompt

This entire game was built from the single prompt below — nothing else.

---

Build a complete, polished browser game in a single HTML file: "ORBITFALL", a neon one-button arcade game. The player is a glowing comet trapped in orbit around a dying star. There are two orbit rings, an inner ring and an outer ring, and one button — Space, click, or tap — flips the comet between them. Shards of debris spiral in from the edge of space toward the star along straight radial paths; dodge them by switching rings at the last moment, because a shard that crosses your ring where you are ends the run. Golden starlight motes appear on the rings: fly through them to score. Passing a shard by a hair's breadth is a near miss and builds a combo multiplier that makes every mote worth more; the combo fades if you stop earning it. The longer you survive, the faster you orbit and the faster and thicker the debris comes. Include a title screen with instructions, a live score and best score, floating score popups, particle effects, a comet trail, screen shake, a pulsing star with a rotating corona, a game-over screen with restart, and tiny synthesized sound effects with no audio files. Dark space background with twinkling stars, a cyan and magenta neon palette, smooth 60 fps canvas rendering, and controls that work with keyboard, mouse, and touch.
